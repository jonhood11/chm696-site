# Lectures

The lecture notes have been migrated from the shared CHM 696 LaTeX and Beamer
project. They are organized in a reusable mathematical sequence rather than by
calendar date.

## Subject sequence

1. Series, limits, and complex numbers
2. Vectors, matrices, linear systems, and eigenvalue problems
3. Optimization and singular value decomposition
4. Regression, clustering, classification, and neural networks
5. Fourier series, transforms, and wavelets
6. Ordinary and partial differential equations
7. Green's functions and Sturm-Liouville theory
8. Complex analysis and the calculus of variations
9. Markov processes and Monte Carlo methods

The companion [computational notebooks](../computations/index.md) use Python to
check calculations and make the central ideas visible.
